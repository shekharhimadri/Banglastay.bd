# Banglastay.com.bd

BanglaStay is a travel and hotel booking platform dedicated to helping travellers discover and book stays across Bangladesh. Inspired by the country's "Golden Bengal" heritage, the site showcases the diversity of Bangladesh's landscapes — from the world's longest natural sandy beach at Cox's Bazar to the cloud-kissed hills of Sajek Valley, the Bengal tiger's mangrove home in the Sundarbans, and the bustling riverfront of Old Dhaka.

What visitors can do:

Search for stays using a search bar with destination, check-in/check-out dates, and guest count.
Explore six featured destinations, each with real photography, a short tagline, the number of available properties, and a starting price in Bangladeshi Taka.
Browse handpicked hotels and lodges, filterable by region, with guest ratings, review counts, amenity tags (sea view, pool, free WiFi, beachfront, and more), and per-night pricing.
Save favourites by tapping the heart icon on any property.
Book a stay through a guided booking flow — pick nights and guests, see a live price breakdown including taxes, and receive a confirmation screen upon booking.
Discover experiences beyond the hotel room, including beach and island trips, hill trails, wildlife safaris, and food trails.
Catch limited-time deals, such as a monsoon-season promotion with up to 30% off.
The design uses a green-and-gold colour palette echoing Bangladesh's flag and landscape, with a responsive layout that works from mobile to desktop, smooth hover animations, and a clean, modern aesthetic suited for production use.
